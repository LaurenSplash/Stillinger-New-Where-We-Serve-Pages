Local context

Columbia investigations, run from our Midlands headquarters.

Columbia is South Carolina’s capital and the center of the Midlands. On any given day, people move between downtown, the University of South Carolina, Fort Jackson, state and county offices, and neighborhoods on both sides of the river for work, school, family obligations, and daily routines.

That movement matters when planning an investigation. A routine that starts in a Richland County neighborhood can include a workplace in West Columbia, an errand in Irmo, and an evening at Lake Murray. Because I-20, I-26, and I-77 all meet in the Columbia area, a single day can cross county lines more than once. Understanding likely routes, schedules, destinations, and vehicles helps determine how an assignment should be approached.

Stillinger Investigations provides personal, legal, insurance, business, surveillance, records, digital, and technical investigative services throughout Columbia and the surrounding Midlands. Our investigators work regularly with Columbia-area family-law and defense firms and are familiar with the Richland and Lexington county courts where many local matters are heard.

Columbia is also home to Stillinger’s corporate office at 1416 Park Street in downtown Columbia. It provides a local point of contact for clients throughout Richland and Lexington counties and coordinates assignments across South Carolina.
